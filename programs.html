War Feather · 2026 // Active
2026 — ongoing

The Year-Long Operating System

A four-phase program for veterans and first responders: Selection → Therapy → Biomarkers → Annual Gathering. 50-person cohort, 12-month term, $15–20K value per participant, zero cost to accepted. Three-phase application is anonymous to the committee.

“Not charity. Not passive help. Programs you earn into.”
