N°02

What happens after
service matters.

// Mission BriefSection · 02

War Feather exists to help veterans and first responders rebuild structure, identity, and direction through a long-term system — not a short-term solution.

The mission doesn't end when service does. The drift starts quietly: a lost rhythm, a missing crew, a body that won't shut down on command.

This is a year-long, four-part operating system designed by people who've been there. Treatment, biomarkers, performance, community — selected for, executed together, accountable end-to-end.

// Where support becomes impact

Not charity. Not passive help. A rigorous selection process for those ready to do the work.

N°05

Four-part
operating system.

// DoctrinePillars · 05
N°01 // Treatment

Psychedelic Therapy

Coming Home Project — partner-led 501(c)(3). Supervised, structured, and integrated into the year-long arc.

N°02 // Biology

Biomarkers & Hormones

Blood panels, peptides, hormone therapy via Transcend. Data-driven recovery for the body that carried the work.

N°03 // Training

Human Performance

Programmed training, sleep, nutrition, and recovery protocols. Built around the manual. Reviewed quarterly.

N°04 // Continuation

Annual Gathering

Once a year, the class regroups. Continuation, accountability, and the next handoff to the cohort behind you.
