// Mission BriefSection · 02

Bird's Eye View Project builds long-term programs that help veterans and first responders rebuild structure, identity, and direction — through action, not theory.

The mission doesn't end when service does. The drift starts quietly: a lost rhythm, a missing crew, a body that won't shut down on command.

We design action-based programs across five wellness pillars — mental, physical, emotional, social, spiritual — and fund them through unconventional events. Skydives. Expeditions. Field operations. The work that earns the support.

// What we do

Not charity. Not passive help. Programs you earn into. Funded by people who show up.

N°05

Four-phase
program.

// DoctrinePhases · 05
N°01 // Selection

Selection

Three-phase application — anonymous review, psych evaluation, physical challenge. Cohort hand-picked on merit, not branch.

N°02 // Coming Home

Therapy

Psychedelic therapy via the Coming Home Project — supervised, structured, integrated into the year-long arc.

N°03 // Transcend · UVA

Biomarkers

Blood panels, peptides, and hormone optimization via Transcend (transitioning to UVA). Data-driven recovery for the body that carried the work.

N°04 // Continuation

Annual Gathering

Once a year, the class regroups. Continuation, accountability, and the next handoff to the cohort behind you.
