// OutcomeFoundation · 03

7X proved that large-scale structured programming can be executed with discipline — across geography, demographics, and clinical partners.

It showed that veterans and first responders respond to high-performance systems, not just treatment alone. The combination of training, recovery, community, and accountability is what produces the change.

Bird's Eye View is the refinement: the same operating principles applied to a smaller, hand-picked cohort, over a longer arc, with deeper community continuation. War Feather is what 7X became when the system got more honest.
