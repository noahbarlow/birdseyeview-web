N°01

What this is.

// Program BriefDefinition · 01

A year-long, four-part operating system to rebuild structure, identity, and direction — for the people who already gave the most to it.

War Feather isn't an outpatient program. It's not a retreat. It's a 52-week structured commitment with treatment, biomarkers, performance protocols, and community — selected for, executed together, accountable end-to-end.

The investment per participant is fifteen to twenty thousand dollars in real value. The cost to those accepted is zero. Your cohort is a maximum of fifty, hand-picked through a three-phase application that judges merit, not branch.

N°02

Four-phase
program.

// DoctrinePhases · 02
N°01 // Cohort Selection

Selection

Three-phase application: anonymous review, psych evaluation, physical challenge. Cohort hand-picked on merit, not military achievement.

N°02 // Coming Home

Therapy

Psychedelic therapy via the Coming Home Project — partner-led 501(c)(3). Supervised, structured, integrated into the year-long arc.

N°03 // Transcend · UVA

Biomarkers

Blood panels, peptides, hormone optimization via Transcend (transitioning to UVA). Data-driven recovery for the body that carried the work.

N°04 // Continuation

Annual Gathering

Once a year, the class regroups. Continuation, accountability, and the next handoff to the cohort behind you.

// The Human Performance Manual runs through all four phases — weekly cadence, training, sleep, nutrition, recovery.

N°03

Program
timeline.

// Phased Arc52 · Weeks
Weeks 1–4 · Treatment

Initial Phase

Psychedelic therapy with Coming Home, full biomarker panel and hormone baseline through Transcend. The body and mind get measured before the work begins.

Weeks 1–48 · System

Weekly Operating Cadence

Monday week-preview, Wednesday manual work, Friday cohort check-in, Sunday personal reflection. Every week. For a year.

Quarterly · In-Person

Cohort Convergence

Every twelve weeks the class assembles in person — performance audits, treatment review, shared training, accountability resets.

Week 52 · Completion

Annual Gathering & Leadership Transition

Graduation event. Each completed participant becomes a team leader for the incoming cohort, with their own fundraising link.
